# Learnings — Slice 14

- Curva Grande and Descida do Lago are full proper names the user wants to hear,
  not generic descriptors to remove. Treat them as explicit exceptions rather
  than broadening or reversing the shortening rule for unrelated corners.
- Existing curated aliases normally win. An exception must override that
  precedence, not merely be another entry in REVIEWED; otherwise Lago 1/2 and
  Grande would survive every preview/write cycle.
- The data contains Descida do Lago 1 and 2, not just the bare name. Preserve
  both suffixes while keeping the entire proper name; test the bare form too.

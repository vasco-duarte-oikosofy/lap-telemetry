## Layout QA (numeric, since visual QA is still pending)

Overlay of this centreline on the TUMFTM-aligned
`silverstone-grand-prix-circuit---elms.json`:

- median **5.1 m**, p90 **7.5 m** — the short loop runs on GP tarmac, so the
  two outlines agree wherever they share road;
- **one contiguous excursion** peaking at **90.9 m** over 29 of 500 samples
  (5.8 % of the lap, ~170 m of road) — the International-only link road that
  the GP layout does not use. That is the expected difference between the two
  layouts, not a misalignment.

Bounding boxes: International `x −467.6..460.7, y −842.0..152.5`; GP
`x −472.0..554.7, y −848.9..870.2` — the short loop sits inside the GP box, as
it must.
